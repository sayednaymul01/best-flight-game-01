Coral Run — a 3D chase-view flight game: fly the island courier through glowing rings, mind your fuel, and land back on the runway. Three sorties (Ring Run, Island Mail, Storm Courier), a full HUD with altitude, speed, heading, throttle and fuel, and a living island of beaches, palms, a mountain and drifting clouds.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Fly with W/S for throttle, arrow keys to bank, climb and dive, or hold the mouse to steer. Press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a mission, Enter to fly, P to pause, R to restart.
